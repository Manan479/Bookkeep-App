# Bookkeep audit — 21 October 2026

> Follow-up: the fixes and latest verification results are recorded in [AUDIT_FIXES_2026-10-21.md](AUDIT_FIXES_2026-10-21.md). The findings below describe the pre-fix audit.

## Delivered in this change

- **Add charge** in sale, purchase, quotation and distributor editors: custom name, amount and optional tax percentage. Charges are saved with the document and included in preview, PDF, CSV line exports and totals. They do not change stock.
- Linking an expense to an invoice prompts **Add charge to invoice / Keep as company cost only / Cancel**. Recovery applies to posted sales invoices; invalid or unavailable targets refuse the entire save.
- Editing a recovered expense updates its existing invoice charge rather than adding a duplicate. Removing a recovered expense removes its invoice charge in the same transaction. Invoice payments/returns can block unsafe changes.
- Supplier-invoice charges are expensed separately from inventory cost. Customer-facing charges are billed as income; the original expense remains a business cost.

## Coverage and limits

Inspected **71 screen scenarios at 1440, 1024 and 390 pixels wide: 213 renders and 828 field instances**. Inventoried visible controls, form values/options, render errors and viewport overflow; exercised salary-slip opening, report Back/scroll restoration, register selections, payment editing, charge posting, linked-expense changes, document exports and financial examples. Source and single-file build tests, restart persistence checks and the existing browser suites were also run.

This is a local audit using disposable sample books, not the user's working book. A field inventory is not proof of every possible input combination. Native Android APK behavior, physical printers, operating-system share targets, live cloud accounts, multi-device conflicts, device permissions and long-term/performance stress are **not verified**. No APK was built. No financial records in the user's book were changed by the audit.

## Outstanding findings, in priority order

### 1. High — invoice discounts are posted against tax rather than reducing revenue

**Confirmed in browser.** Create a sale of 100 with a 10 amount discount and no tax. The customer total is 90, but Sales increases by 100 and Tax payable decreases by 10. Expected Sales +90 and Tax payable unchanged. A balanced journal can still classify amounts incorrectly.

Source: `web/src/features/invoices.js` passes `T.sub` to `postSaleLedger`; `web/src/core/posting.js` derives tax as total minus subtotal. Fix net revenue and explicit tax/rounding allocation together, then plan how to correct existing entries.

### 2. High — fractional totals disagree between saved documents and the register/ledger

**Confirmed in browser.** A charge/invoice of 100.49 saves `T.g = 100.49` but the register stores 100. `Math.round` in invoice posting discards currency fractions. This affects balances, payments and exports. Use one currency precision policy throughout posting and presentation; rounding needs an explicit rule/account if required.

### 3. High — creating some financial records is not atomic

**Source-confirmed recovery gap; storage-failure scenarios were not exhaustively exercised here.** Return creation (`saveRet`), manual-journal creation (`postJournal`), salary creation (`postSalary`), staff payments/advances and account transfers directly mutate records, stock, balances or ledger in separate steps. Their edit/removal paths have more transaction protection than their creation paths. A refused ledger entry or failed storage write can leave partially changed state. Route the entire operation through `bookTransaction`, including counters and stock layers.

### 4. High — some document text is interpreted as HTML

**Confirmed in browser.** Put `<b id="audit-description-markup">This should be plain text</b>` in an item description. Preview creates a real HTML element. Names, notes and other fields also have raw template interpolation paths. This permits unintended markup and presents an injection risk when importing/sharing books. This check demonstrated HTML interpretation, not an exploit. Escape user text consistently while preserving generated document markup.

### 5. Medium — short A4 documents still overflow to a second PDF page

**Confirmed in all three styles.** A sample short purchase invoice measured 794 × 1160 px in Classic, 794 × 1158 in Clean and 794 × 1166 in Bold, versus an A4 page height of 1123 px. Each exported to **two PDF pages**. The signing area is present at 160 px; the complete header/body/footer layout still exceeds one page. This is an outstanding defect, despite the earlier fixes to height rounding. Rebalance margins, header sizing and footer layout without shrinking the promised signature space.

### 6. Medium — wide table PDF exports exceed paper width

**Confirmed in browser.** The detailed invoice table has 17 columns; measured table width was 854 px on a 794 px paper. The usable content area is smaller still. Table export needs a landscape/wider-page layout or column grouping/wrapping. Full invoice PDFs are a separate path.

### 7. Medium — typing in several searches loses keyboard focus

**Confirmed for Expenses.** Type `Rent` normally: only `R` remains because the input event replaces the active element. Sales, Inventory and Payments use similar whole-screen redraw handlers and require the same check. Preserve focus/caret or update only the result rows. The newer `registerSearch` helper already restores focus.

### 8. Medium — invoice terms are unreadable in dark mode

**Confirmed in browser.** Template terms textarea computes black text (`rgb(0,0,0)`) on a near-black background (`rgb(16,25,22)`). Set foreground/background tokens on textarea controls. Editing two newline-separated terms correctly produces two terms; the defect here is contrast.

### 9. Medium — CSV text is not protected against spreadsheet formulas

**Confirmed exporter output; Excel execution was not tested.** `csvCell('=1+1')` returns `=1+1`. A spreadsheet may interpret party names/notes starting with formula characters as formulas. Quoting commas and quotes does not prevent this. Define a safe spreadsheet-text export policy for text columns, while retaining numeric columns as numbers.

### 10. Medium — return actions promise more than they support

**Source-confirmed.** Every nonvoid return is blocked by `registerRemovalProblem`, not just older imported return notes. The UI still offers Delete / Void. Editing only permits notes. Provide an explicit supported-action message and implement stock/settlement-aware reversal before offering general removal.

### 11. Medium — manual cash journals cannot be edited

**Source-confirmed.** `editManualJournal` refuses records with a `cash` mapping and asks for reversal/re-entry. Automatic journals correctly direct editing to the source document, but manual cash journals still show Edit. Reconstruct the original per-account lines, or clearly indicate the restriction before opening an action.

### 12. Medium — record actions are inconsistent outside the main registers

**Source-confirmed.** Employee payments (`STFP`), employee advances (`EMPA`) and cash transfers do not have the same complete detail/edit/void/action paths as main Payments/Expenses. They are separate record types, so editing general Payments does not cover them. Add safe dedicated paths, dependency checks and audit history.

### 13. Low — long text is truncated in image/table exports

**Source-confirmed.** `tableImage` caps each column width and replaces long cell text with an ellipsis. The resulting image does not contain the complete value; details may be lost when the image is shared. Wrap text or clearly separate compact summary images from full-detail exports.

### 14. Low — scanner control is a placeholder

**Source-confirmed.** Invoice `scan()` only displays “Scanner opens here.” The product-search placeholder also mentions scanning. Implement the scanner and desktop fallback, or remove the implied working feature.

### 15. Low — many icon controls lack descriptive accessible names

**Field/control inventory and source review.** Back, export, add, preview and other icon-only controls frequently rely on glyphs without an `aria-label`. Some newer controls have labels, but the pattern is inconsistent. Audit buttons by purpose, including modal controls and disabled states.

### 16. Low — terminology and translations remain inconsistent

**Source review.** Examples include “advance back,” “Run them now,” abbreviations such as “adv,” and many English strings outside the translation dictionary. Use explicit salary/recovery labels, consistent action wording and translated strings. Urdu/RTL end-to-end editing was not validated in this run.

## Issues found and corrected during this change

- Removing a recovered expense originally left an orphaned customer charge. It now removes that charge inside the same transaction; the regression check passes.
- Charge-only invoices were initially subject to product-stock checks. Charges are now excluded from stock blocking and stock mutations.
- Non-stock charges would have appeared as products in the sales-by-product report. That report now excludes charge lines.
- Product returns now exclude charge lines, with return quantity controls retaining the correct original item index.
- Charge validation also applies to quotations before posting.
- The old test harness and several assertions expected UI behavior that has since changed (Export list wording, no expense PDF, automatic invoice accent). The harness/assertions were updated; these were test-tool mismatches, not additional user-facing bugs.
